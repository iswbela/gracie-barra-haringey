Our Story

More Than
A Gym.
A Family.

Gracie Barra Haringey is part of the world's largest Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu organisation — founded by Master Carlos Gracie Jr. We bring that global standard of excellence to North London, creating a safe, welcoming environment where everyone from complete beginners to seasoned competitors can grow.

Our academy runs structured programmes for all ages and levels. Whether you're looking for fitness, self-defence, competition or simply a challenge — this is your place.

Your Goals

Every programme is structured with the Gracie Barra curriculum — giving you a clear path from your first class to black belt.

01
🥋

Adults BJJ

Ages 16+

Our flagship programme covers all aspects of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — takedowns, guard passing, submissions and self-defence. Suitable for all levels.

  • Fundamentals & Advanced tracks
  • Gi & No-Gi classes
  • Live sparring (rolling)
  • Competition preparation
02
👶

Kids & Teens

Ages 4–15

Build confidence, discipline and resilience. Our GB Kids programme uses age-appropriate methods to develop character alongside technique.

  • Little Champions (4–6 yrs)
  • Kids (7–12 yrs)
  • Teens (13–15 yrs)
  • Anti-bullying focus
03
💪

No-Gi / Fitness

All Levels

Grappling without the kimono. Fast-paced, athletic and a phenomenal workout. Perfect for MMA athletes and those who prefer shorts over gi.

  • Wrestling & leg locks
  • High-intensity drilling
  • Crossfit-style warm-ups
  • Great standalone workout
